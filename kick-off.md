<summary><h3>13:00–14:00 | Type & Move Exercises</h3></summary>

**Analog exercise in groups of 3–4.** One pencil, one sheet of paper, several bodies.

**Material:** large paper (A2 / A1), tape, one pencil / marker / brush per group, string (optional), movement cards.

1. **Holding together [10min]**
   - Everyone in the group holds the same pencil at the same time.
   - Draw a letter together without talking. Nobody leads. Where does the line go?
2. **Describe / show [15min]**
   - One person stands away from the paper and _shows_ a movement with their body (no words).
   - The others translate it, each on their own paper.
   - Second round: One person _shows_ a movement to only one person. That person _describes_ the movement with words (no gestures) to the others in the group that haven’t seen the motion. What changes?
3. **Movement visualizations [15min]**
   - Each write down 5-10 words describing movement.
   - One person now reads out one word, the others each draw a letter in that motion on a paper.
   - _e.g. sweep, tremble, spiral, bounce, hesitate, fall, push, stretch, scratch, float, stumble, repeat_
   - You can also combine two words: _"slow + tremble"_, _"bounce + spiral"_
4. **Movement → Letter [10min]**
   - Pick a movement (e.g. from the word list from exercise 3). Now use that movement to draw a stroke. For example, if your movement is fast, you should move your pen fast. If your movement is jumpy, you have to jump while drawing.
   - Pick different letters. Now draw the letters only through those movement.
5. **Lay out & look [10min]**
   - Put all sheets on the floor. Which marks were made by which movement? Which describe a movement better – words or bodies?

</details>

<summary><h3>14:00–16:00 | Group Formation</h3></summary>

1. **Interest-Mapping [30min]**
   - Fill out the following questions on post-its:
     - movement
       - _What kind of movement interests you?_ (hand, body, dance, everyday gestures, machines, nature, ...)
     - medium
       - _Analog / digital / both?_ (drawing, painting, printing, p5.js, body-tracking, Glyphs, animation, video, installation, ...)
     - topic
       - _What do you want to explore? Are there any overlaps?_
     - skill-share
       - _What skills do you need / want to learn?_
       - _What skills could you teach to others?_
2. **Finding groups [30min]**
   - Add your name in the corner of the post-it. Now map yourselves within the group by creating a shared-post-it map.
   - Look for overlaps and clusters on the map. Talk to people with similar (or opposite) interests.
   - Find a group of 2-4 people. Optimally, we have 10 groups or less.
3. **First group sketch [60min]**
   - In your group, write down first ideas or questions.
   - Make 3 quick sketches (analog or digital) and pin them up.
4. **Wrap-up**
   - Short round: each group presents in 1 sentence.
